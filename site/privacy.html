Tenet / Privacy

Privacy

Tenet is a demo of buyer protection for stablecoin payments on testnets: Arc testnet for the money and GenLayer Bradbury for the judges. Testnet USDC has no value. This page says what the site and its Demo Shop keep about you, and what they do not. Last updated 2026-10-16.

Your wallet

When you connect a wallet, the page reads your wallet address and your balances on Arc testnet. Every transaction you sign (payment, claim, withdrawal) is public on the blockchain, like any other transaction there.

Sign in with Google (Circle wallet)

If you choose to continue with Google, the sign-in is handled by Google and by Circle, which creates a wallet on Arc testnet that you control. Tenet never receives your Google password. Circle gives the page a session token so you can approve the Demo Shop transactions in Circle's window; the Demo Shop service passes that token to Circle for each transaction you approve and does not store it. Tenet does not store your name, email address or Google profile.

What the Demo Shop keeps

  • For each order: its number, the scenario, your wallet address, the order's transactions on Arc and GenLayer, and the emails the demo store signs for it. This is what runs the demo and shows your orders.
  • My orders shows every order your wallet paid, read from the Demo Shop by your wallet address (the payments are public on Arc), so you can find them from any browser. Your browser also keeps the list of orders you started on this device, including unpaid ones; clearing this site's data in your browser removes that list.
  • No cookies are used for tracking, and there are no ads or analytics.

Services the site uses

The site and the Demo Shop service run on Cloudflare, which processes requests (including IP addresses) to serve them. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Reads of Arc testnet go to Arc's public RPC.
